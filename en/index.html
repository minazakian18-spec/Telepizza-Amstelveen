Welcome to our home

Since 1984 we have opened our doors every afternoon on the Amsterdamseweg. Here you are not a number: we remember your favourite pizza, push tables together for your birthday and stay open until you are done talking. Whether you live around the corner or came from the other side of the world: pull up a chair, you are welcome.

From takeaway pizzeria to the night out in Amstelveen

Telepizza started in 1984 as a pizzeria on the Amsterdamseweg. Today we are a lively Italian restaurant with a green wall, neon lights, great cocktails and a kitchen where everything is made fresh, from hand-stretched pizza bases and rich sauces to juicy steaks from the grill.

Your Italian restaurant in Amstelveen

Fresh pizza in Amstelveen

Looking for the best pizza in Amstelveen? We make every pizza with hand-stretched dough, homemade tomato sauce and generous toppings. Choose a classic margherita or pepperoni, our signature Telepizza, truffle with Parma ham, or build your own. Vegetarian, seafood or halal meat: there are over 35 pizzas to choose from.

Pasta, steaks, kapsalon and burgers

Besides pizza we serve pasta such as carbonara, bolognese and frutti di mare, steaks from the grill, spare ribs, the Dutch favourite kapsalon, shawarma and burgers. All our meat is halal and we are open until 1:45 am, on Friday and Saturday until 3 am.

FAQ

Where can I get good pizza in Amstelveen?

At Telepizza, Amsterdamseweg 452, 1181 BW Amstelveen. We have been making fresh pizza since 1984. Dine in, take away or have it delivered.

Is the meat halal?

Yes. All our meat is halal, including the shawarma, chicken shawarma and döner.

What are your opening hours?

Tuesday to Thursday and Sunday from 3 pm to 1:45 am, Friday and Saturday until 3 am. Closed on Mondays.

Do you deliver in Amstelveen?

Yes, we deliver across Amstelveen. Order online via our ordering page.

Can I book a table?

Yes, book online via TheFork or call 020-6435287.
